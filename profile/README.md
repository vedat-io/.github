# Vedat LLC

Data curation, analytics, and packaging. Public data, made usable.

Vedat LLC is an Illinois company. Our first product is **FCA-CF** (Federal Contract Awards by Corporate Family): Lockheed Martin’s U.S. federal contract awards for FY2023–FY2025, mapped across the corporate family (91,042 awards, $195.5 billion in obligations, 131 UEIs in the family table). The raw records are free on USAspending.gov; the packet’s value is the family mapping, the methods, and the license. It covers companies and government contracts, not private individuals.
